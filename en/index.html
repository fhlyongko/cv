Research Overview

My research examines the intersection of English language education and artificial intelligence. Using surveys, case studies, and mixed-methods approaches, I investigate how university students and pre-service teachers perceive, adopt, and use generative AI in learning and teaching. Drawing on my graduate training in bilingual education and ESOL in the United States, I also examine English-medium instruction (EMI) and educational support for culturally and linguistically diverse learners.

AI Literacy

AI literacy among university students and pre-service teachers, disciplinary variation, and the relationship between prior learning experiences and perceptions of AI.

Generative AI in English Language Education

Generative AI in general English and English writing courses, with a focus on learning strategies, motivation, cognitive load, and satisfaction.

English-Medium Instruction (EMI)

International students’ experiences in graduate-level EMI programs and approaches that recognize multilingualism as an educational resource.

Pre-Service Teacher Education

The development of teaching self-efficacy through microteaching, flipped learning, and ESL field experiences.

Bilingual and Multicultural Education

Korean heritage language schools in the United States, Korean–Chinese bilingual instruction, and raciolinguistic ideologies in language-in-education policy.

Global Competence

Global competence development and curriculum design in undergraduate programs for students exploring their choice of major.
