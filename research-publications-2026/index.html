01 / Academic Profile

Yong-Jik Lee

Assistant Professor, Changwon National University
School of Sarim-Honors

My research examines the integration of artificial intelligence into English language education, with particular attention to learner experiences, writing practices, and teacher development. Related areas of inquiry include English-medium instruction, international students’ learning experiences, and multimedia learning.

Across these areas, I investigate how instructional approaches and digital technologies relate to learning and teaching in school and university settings.

This catalogue presents the 26 research articles included in the supplied list of publications coauthored by Yong-Jik Lee and Robert Davis. It represents the scope of that list rather than a complete career bibliography.
