01 / About

Yong-Jik Lee

Assistant Professor, Changwon National University
School of Sarim-Honors

My research interests include AI-assisted English language learning, teacher education and professional development, English-medium instruction, and multimedia learning.

영어교육과 AI 활용을 중심으로 교사교육, 영어매개수업(EMI), 국제학생의 학습 경험, 멀티미디어 학습을 연구합니다.
